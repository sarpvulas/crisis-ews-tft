Scope and exceptions
--------------------

This archive contains the source code for the MSc dissertation "Dense Drawdown
Supervision for Equity Crisis Early Warning: A Multi-Task Temporal Fusion
Transformer and an Honest Test of Regime Awareness" (MSc Computational Finance,
King's College London, 2025/2026; supervisor: Dr Bart de Keijzer).

Third-party libraries. Well-known, freely obtainable third-party libraries
(PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, and the remaining
packages pinned in requirements/) are NOT included in this archive. They are
declared in requirements/repro.txt and requirements/cluster.txt and are
referenced in the report.

Prior work implemented from the literature. The Temporal Fusion Transformer
backbone is my own implementation of the architecture described by Lim et al.
(2021), and the regime-switching and focal-loss components follow the
formulations of Hamilton (1989) / Ang and Bekaert (2002) and Lin et al. (2017)
respectively. All such sources are cited in the report. The implementations
themselves are my own.

Data. The market series underlying this project are daily Bloomberg data and
are licensed separately; no Bloomberg data is included in this archive. The
included datasets under data/mock/ are mock surrogates I generated (see the
README's Data & licensing section); they are unusable as market data and
exist solely so the pipeline can be executed. See LICENSE and the report's
Data section.
